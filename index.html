MLSynd Footy Tipping Competition Rules

1. Season

The competition covers the full AFL home-and-away season plus the entire finals series (including the Grand Final).

2. Scoring

1 point is awarded for each correct tip.

Draws only score a point if the tipper specifically selected "Draw". Otherwise the tip scores zero.

A Perfect Round bonus of +2 points is awarded when a tipper correctly tips every game in a round (minimum 8 games). This applies in both the home-and-away season and finals.

If tips are not submitted, the Away team is automatically allocated for scoring purposes. No Perfect Round bonus can be earned on a round containing auto-tips.

3. Joker Round

Each tipper may nominate one round per season as their Joker Round.

In their chosen Joker Round, all points they score that round (including any Perfect Round bonus) are doubled.

Multiple tippers can choose the same round as their Joker — it's not limited to one person per round.

The Joker Round can be nominated or changed at any time, provided it's before the opening bounce of the first game in that round. Once that round has started, the Joker locks in and can't be changed.

If a tipper doesn't nominate a Joker Round, they simply forfeit the double-points opportunity.

4. Deadlines

Each game locks individually, 12 hours before its own opening bounce — not the whole round at once. You can freely change your pick for any game right up until that 12-hour window begins, even if earlier games in the same round have already started.

Once a game enters its lockout window, no changes are allowed for that game.

5. Prize Distribution

The total prize pool is split as follows:

1st place – 70%
2nd place – 20%
3rd place – 10%
